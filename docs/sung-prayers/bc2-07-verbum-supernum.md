## The text

Latin after Dreves, with capitals for the screen. English ours.

**1**
> Verbum supernum prodiens,
> nec Patris linquens dexteram,
> ad opus suum exiens
> venit ad vitae vesperam.

*The Word from on high, going forth / yet not leaving the Father's right hand, / setting out to do his work, / came to the evening of his life.*

**2**
> In mortem a discipulo
> suis tradendus aemulis,
> prius in vitae ferculo
> se tradidit discipulis.

*About to be handed over by a disciple / to his enemies, to die, / he first, as the food of life, / handed himself over to his disciples.*

**3**
> Quibus sub bina specie
> carnem dedit et sanguinem,
> ut duplicis substantiae
> totum cibaret hominem.

*To them, under two kinds, / he gave his flesh and his blood, / to feed the whole of man, / who is made of body and soul.*

**4**
> Se nascens dedit socium,
> convescens in edulium,
> se moriens in pretium,
> se regnans dat in praemium.

*By his birth he gave himself as our companion; / at table, as our food; / by his death, as our ransom; / reigning, he gives himself as our reward.*

**5**
> O salutaris hostia,
> quae caeli pandis ostium,
> bella premunt hostilia:
> da robur, fer auxilium.

*O saving Victim, / who open wide the gate of heaven: / hostile wars press upon us; / give strength, bring help.*

**6**
> Uni trinoque Domino
> sit sempiterna gloria,
> qui vitam sine termino
> nobis donet in patria. Amen.

*To the Lord, One and Three, / be everlasting glory: / may he give us life without end / in our true homeland. Amen.*

Notes for the description:
- Stanza 2 turns on the word *tradere*, "to hand over": Judas hands him over to death, but he has already handed himself over to his disciples as food.
- Stanza 3: *duplicis substantiae*, "of twofold substance", means body and soul. Christ gives both his flesh and his blood so that he feeds the whole person.
- Stanza 4 is the hymn's summary: companion, food, ransom, reward.
- Dreves prints no Amen; the Amen is the usual liturgical close of a hymn. Credit it as added if you keep it (as with 03).

## Lyrics (for Suno or Gemini)

About 3 minutes at a slow pace, so it fits Gemini as well.

```
[Intro: soft drone, single bell]

[Verse 1: solo voice, almost unaccompanied]
Verbum supernum prodiens,
nec Patris linquens dexteram,
ad opus suum exiens
venit ad vitae vesperam.

[Verse 2]
In mortem a discipulo
suis tradendus aemulis,
prius in vitae ferculo
se tradidit discipulis.

[Verse 3: bass enters]
Quibus sub bina specie
carnem dedit et sanguinem,
ut duplicis substantiae
totum cibaret hominem.

[Verse 4: warmer]
Se nascens dedit socium,
convescens in edulium,
se moriens in pretium,
se regnans dat in praemium.

[Instrumental: flugelhorn answers]

[Verse 5: hushed, adoring]
O salutaris hostia,
quae caeli pandis ostium,
bella premunt hostilia:
da robur, fer auxilium.

[Verse 6: fullest, then still]
Uni trinoque Domino
sit sempiterna gloria,
qui vitam sine termino
nobis donet in patria.
Amen.

[End: drone fades, one bell]
```
No repeats, no other added words.

**If Suno flags the lyrics:** *O salutaris* is among the most recorded texts in Latin, so a flag is likely. Use the same text split into syllables, as for 03. Ask me for it.
