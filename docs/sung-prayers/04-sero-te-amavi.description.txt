Sero te amavi ("Too late have I loved you"): St. Augustine's prayer from the tenth book of his Confessions, in an original contemplative setting for Ash Wednesday. Sung in Latin, with E. B. Pusey's 1838 English on screen.

Augustine wrote the Confessions around the year 400, a bishop looking back on the years he spent searching for God everywhere but within. This short passage is its heart: "Thou wert with me, but I was not with Thee."
