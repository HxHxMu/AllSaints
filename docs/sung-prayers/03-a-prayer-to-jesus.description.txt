"O Blessed Jesus, my tender Redeemer": a prayer St. Elizabeth Ann Seton wrote in her own hand, set to original contemplative music for her feast day. Every word is hers, sung in her own English.

Widow, mother of five, convert, teacher and foundress of the Sisters of Charity at Emmitsburg, Maryland, she was the first person born in the United States to be canonized (1975). The prayer survives on an undated sheet in the archives of her sisters; it confesses a faith that feels "imperfect" and asks Jesus to "accept what I do for what I would do if I were able."
