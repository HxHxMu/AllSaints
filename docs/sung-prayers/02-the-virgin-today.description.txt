"Today the Virgin gives birth to the One beyond all being": the Christmas hymn of St. Romanos the Melodist, deacon of Constantinople, in an original contemplative setting. Sung twice through in Greek, with our English on screen.

These six lines open his first hymn on the Nativity, written in the sixth century. Byzantine churches still sing them every Christmas. Tradition holds that the Mother of God gave Romanos the gift of song in a dream on Christmas Eve, and that he woke and sang this hymn.
