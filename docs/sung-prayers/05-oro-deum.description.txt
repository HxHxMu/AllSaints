Oro Deum ("I pray God"): St. Patrick's prayer for perseverance, from his Confession, in an original contemplative setting for his feast day. Sung in Latin, with N. J. D. White's 1920 English on screen.

Patrick wrote the Confession in old age, a bishop in Ireland, the land where he had once been a slave, giving an account of his life to God. Near its end he asks for one thing: "I pray God to give me perseverance."
