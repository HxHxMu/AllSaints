# Blue Chant, Volume II · Hymns of the Saints

Draft, 2026-11-14. One long-form music video, like Volume I, not tied to liturgical dates. Each track: Latin **words written by a saint** (or plainly marked "attributed"), in a **new melody** (Suno or Gemini/Lyria, from lyrics + style) over Luis's Gregorian-jazz production. Same standard as the sung prayers: Latin from a named public-domain edition, public-domain English on screen, honest credit.

**Credit wording:** "Words: St. ___ (dates). New musical setting composed and produced by Luis E. Bonilla for All Saints, made with AI tools (Suno / Google Gemini)." Never "traditional chant": these are not the chant melodies.

## Why new melodies (Nov 14)

- Suno refuses uploads of the real chant melodies: 12 of 14 guide tracks flagged "matches an existing recording" (*Alma Redemptoris*, then 01, 03–07, 09, B1–B3, B5); support can't override.
- Gemini (Lyria 3.5) takes the score as an image but doesn't follow it: its *Pange lingua* pitch line matched the score no better than chance (`work/sung/bc2/gemini/05-melody-check.png`). Lyria songs top out ~3 min.
- Real melodies would need a singing synthesizer or a live singer; parked.

The guides and scores stay in `work/sung/bc2/guides/` in case that changes.
