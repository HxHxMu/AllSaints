# Sung chants · ten well-known Gregorian chants, Dec 2026 – Nov 2027

Draft, 2026-11-11. A second series next to the sung prayers ([calendar.md](calendar.md)): the **real chant melodies**, sung in Latin over Luis's Gregorian-jazz arrangement, each on the day the Church sings it. Same lyric-video, teaser (Story) and Facebook setup as the sung prayers. Numbered **C01–C10** so they don't collide with the prayer numbers.

Why: the Gregorian *Ave Maria* Short (Blue Chant I) drew 836 views in ~11 h, about 3× a saint Short's lifetime median. People search for, and recognise, the chants themselves.

## How these differ from the sung prayers

| | Sung prayers (01–10) | Sung chants (C01–C10) |
|---|---|---|
| Melody | Original (Suno + Luis) | **The traditional chant melody** (public domain), arranged |
| Credit | "Composed and produced by Luis E. Bonilla…" | "**Traditional Gregorian chant, arranged and produced by Luis E. Bonilla** for All Saints, made with AI tools (Suno)". Never "original" (see Blue Chant I) |
| Words | Written by a canonized saint | Liturgical text; author named only where it's known, "attributed" / "tradition holds" where it isn't |

**Getting the real melody into Suno:** typed lyrics alone get a new Suno tune, not the chant. Suno needs an audio reference (upload → cover / extend). Don't upload someone else's recording (Solesmes etc. are copyrighted, even if the melody isn't). Two clean options: Luis plays or sings the line himself, or Claude renders a plain guide track (piano/organ, free rhythm) from the public-domain square-note melody (*Liber Usualis*), one file per chant.

## Clashes with the prayer calendar (decide before Feb)

1. **Holy Thursday, Mar 25:** prayer 06 is planned as an *original* setting of *Pange lingua*. Proposal: make 06 the real chant instead (C03 replaces 06). Aquinas wrote it, so it fits both series.
2. **Pentecost, May 16:** prayer 09 is Hildegard's *O ignis Spiritus*. Proposal: *Veni Creator* on Pentecost (far better known); Hildegard moves to **Fri Sep 17**, her feast (already listed there as a reshare).
3. **Corpus Christi, May 30:** the calendar's "reshare 06 *Tantum ergo*" goes away; C07 takes the day.
4. **Load:** with these, Dec 24 → Jan 4 has three releases (02, C01, 03) and Mar 17 → May 30 has nine. Too much? Then C04 and C05 can move to 2028.

## Per chant, before the sheet

- Latin text from a public-domain edition (*Liber Usualis* 1908 or the 1912 *Graduale*), and which melody/tone (e.g. Salve Regina simple vs. solemn).
- Public-domain English, page checked; where only a loose paraphrase exists, say "paraphrase" on screen, as with Neale on 07.
- Art: a Byzantine icon fitting the day (Theotokos for the Marian antiphons, the Resurrection for C04, Pentecost for C06), Nano Banana prompts in each sheet.
