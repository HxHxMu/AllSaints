Pange lingua gloriosi ("Sing, my tongue, the Saviour's glory"): St. Thomas Aquinas's hymn to the Body and Blood of Christ, in an original contemplative setting for Holy Thursday. Sung in Latin, with Edward Caswall's 1849 English on screen.

Thomas wrote it for the Office of Corpus Christi, the feast Pope Urban IV instituted in 1264. On Holy Thursday it is sung at the end of the Mass of the Lord's Supper, as the Blessed Sacrament is carried to the altar of repose. Its last two stanzas, the Tantum ergo, are sung at Benediction to this day.
