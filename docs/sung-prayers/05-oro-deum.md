## The text

Latin as printed, with sentence capitals added for the screen. **Bold** = stressed syllable (ecclesiastical Latin).

### 1 (§57)
> **Un**de **au**tem retri**bu**am **il**li pro **om**nibus quae re**tri**buit **mi**hi.

*Wherefore then, I shall render unto him for all his benefits towards me.*

### 2 (§57)
> Sed quid **di**cam uel quid pro**mit**tam **Do**mino **me**o? **Qui**a **ni**hil **ua**leo **ni**si **ip**se **mi**hi de**de**rit.

*But what shall I say, or what shall I promise to my Lord? For I am only worth what he himself has given to me.*

### 3 (§58)
> **O**ro **De**um ut det **mi**hi perseue**ran**tiam, et dig**ne**tur ut **red**dam **il**li testi**mo**nium fi**de**le **us**que ad **tran**situm **me**um **prop**ter **De**um **me**um.

*I pray God to give me perseverance, and to vouchsafe that I bear to him faithful witness, until my passing hence, for the sake of my God.*

White's first English sentence is stiff (the Latin *Unde* is "whence": he is quoting Psalm 116:12, "What shall I render unto the Lord"). It stays as printed: we don't polish a PD translation and keep its name on it.

## Suno

**Style:**
```
contemplative gregorian jazz, solo male chant vocal in latin, plainchant phrasing, dorian mode, low drone, upright bass, soft rhodes, low whistle answering the voice, stone church reverb, steadfast, quiet courage, lent, slow and spacious, no drum groove, no pop chorus
```

**Lyrics (Latin):** the vocal sheet uses *v* (vel, valeo, perseverantiam) so Suno doesn't sing "you-el"; the screen keeps White's *u*. Same words.
```
[Intro: low drone, low whistle]

[Verse 1: solo voice, almost unaccompanied]
Unde autem retribuam illi
pro omnibus quae retribuit mihi.

[Verse 2: bass enters]
Sed quid dicam vel quid promittam
Domino meo?
Quia nihil valeo
nisi ipse mihi dederit.

[Instrumental: low whistle answers]

[Verse 3: the prayer, fuller]
Oro Deum ut det mihi perseverantiam,
et dignetur ut reddam illi
testimonium fidele
usque ad transitum meum
propter Deum meum.

[Instrumental: rhodes and whistle, quiet]

[Outro: the prayer again, softer]
Oro Deum ut det mihi perseverantiam,
et dignetur ut reddam illi
testimonium fidele
usque ad transitum meum
propter Deum meum.

[End: drone fades]
```
The outro repeats his §58 sentence whole and unaltered (no added words). Aim for ~3:30–4:30, like 04.
