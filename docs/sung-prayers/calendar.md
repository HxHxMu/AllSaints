## Suno style prompts

Base sound for all of them: contemplative "Gregorian jazz", free-rhythm chant vocal, no drum groove, no pop chorus. What changes is voice, language, mode, lead instrument and mood. Suno lyrics live in each release's sheet (written from the checked text, never from memory); 01 and 02 have them now.
