Veni, redemptor gentium ("Come, Redeemer of the nations"): the Advent hymn St. Ambrose of Milan wrote for his people in Milan, in an original contemplative setting. All eight of his stanzas are sung in Latin, with J. M. Neale's 1851 English on screen.

St. Augustine quoted it as Ambrose's in a sermon to his own congregation, who had just sung it; Pope Celestine I quoted it in 430. Sixteen centuries later it is still sung in Advent.
