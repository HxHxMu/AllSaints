## The text

Latin after Pitra, with capitals for the screen. English ours.

**1**
> Ave, generosa,
> gloriosa et intacta puella.
> Tu pupilla castitatis,
> tu materia sanctitatis,
> quae Deo placuit.

*Hail, noble one, / glorious and untouched maiden! / You are the pupil of the eye of chastity, / you are the very substance of holiness / that pleased God.*

**2**
> Nam haec superna infusio
> in te fuit,
> quod supernum Verbum
> in te carnem induit.

*For this heavenly outpouring / was in you: / that the Word from on high / put on flesh in you.*

**3**
> Tu candidum lilium,
> quod Deus ante omnem creaturam inspexit.

*You are the white lily / on which God gazed before all creation.*

**4**
> O pulcherrima et dulcissima,
> quam valde Deus in te delectabatur,
> cum amplexionem caloris sui
> in te posuit,
> ita quod Filius eius
> de te lactatus est.

*O most beautiful and most sweet, / how greatly God delighted in you / when he placed the embrace of his warmth / within you, / so that his Son / was nursed by you.*

**5**
> Venter enim tuus gaudium habuit,
> cum omnis caelestis symphonia
> de te sonuit,
> quia, Virgo, Filium Dei portasti,
> ubi castitas tua in Deo claruit.

*For your womb held joy / when all the harmony of heaven / sounded from you, / because, Virgin, you carried the Son of God, / and your chastity shone bright in God.*

**6**
> Viscera tua gaudium habuerunt,
> sicut gramen super quod ros cadit,
> cum ei viriditatem infudit;
> ut et in te factum est,
> o Mater omnis gaudii.

*Your inmost being held joy, / like the grass on which the dew falls / when it pours greenness into it: / so it was done in you, / O Mother of all joy.*

**7**
> Nunc omnis Ecclesia
> in gaudio rutilet
> ac in symphonia sonet,
> propter dulcissimam Virginem
> et laudabilem Mariam,
> Dei Genitricem.
> Amen.

*Now let the whole Church / glow red with joy / and resound in harmony / for the most sweet Virgin, / Mary, worthy of praise, / the Mother of God. / Amen.*

Notes for the description:
- *Viriditas* ("greenness", stanza 6) is Hildegard's signature word for God's life-giving power in creation.
- *Symphonia* (stanzas 5 and 7) is also the name of her own collection of songs.
- The *Amen* is Hildegard's own; Pitra prints it.

## Style

Hildegard wrote her songs for her nuns, so a woman's voice suits this track (the other tracks so far are male). That's your call.

```
contemplative gregorian jazz, solo female voice in latin, soaring free melismatic lines with wide leaps, plainchant phrasing, free rhythm, lydian light, high soft drone, harp-like rhodes arpeggios, upright bass, flugelhorn answering the voice, gentle chimes, vast abbey reverb, tender, luminous, joyful wonder, green spring morning, slow, no drum groove, no pop chorus
```

## Lyrics (for Suno or Gemini)

Not metrical, so the sections follow Hildegard's strophes. About 3–4 minutes at a slow pace. For Gemini, ask for "about 3 minutes" and check that strophe 7 and the Amen made it in.

```
[Intro: high drone, soft chimes]

[Verse 1: solo voice, almost unaccompanied]
Ave, generosa,
gloriosa et intacta puella.
Tu pupilla castitatis,
tu materia sanctitatis,
quae Deo placuit.

[Verse 2]
Nam haec superna infusio
in te fuit,
quod supernum Verbum
in te carnem induit.

[Verse 3: hushed]
Tu candidum lilium,
quod Deus ante omnem creaturam inspexit.

[Verse 4: bass enters, warmer]
O pulcherrima et dulcissima,
quam valde Deus in te delectabatur,
cum amplexionem caloris sui
in te posuit,
ita quod Filius eius
de te lactatus est.

[Instrumental: flugelhorn answers]

[Verse 5: rising]
Venter enim tuus gaudium habuit,
cum omnis caelestis symphonia
de te sonuit,
quia, Virgo, Filium Dei portasti,
ubi castitas tua in Deo claruit.

[Verse 6: tender]
Viscera tua gaudium habuerunt,
sicut gramen super quod ros cadit,
cum ei viriditatem infudit;
ut et in te factum est,
o Mater omnis gaudii.

[Verse 7: fullest, radiant]
Nunc omnis Ecclesia
in gaudio rutilet
ac in symphonia sonet,
propter dulcissimam Virginem
et laudabilem Mariam,
Dei Genitricem.
Amen.

[End: drone fades, one chime]
```
No repeats, no added words.
