## Corrections to the research catalogue

1. **8 stanzas, not 7.** The oldest manuscripts and Fontaine's critical edition open with *Intende, qui regis Israel*; most copies from the 9th century on drop it and start at *Veni, redemptor gentium*. Fontaine and Walpole both judge the first stanza authentic. We sing all 8.
2. **No doxology.** The closing "All honour, laud, and glory be…" in Neale (and *Deo Patri sit gloria…* in breviaries) is a later liturgical addition, not Ambrose. Leave it out, so every word we sing is his.
3. **Don't use the hymnal English.** "Come, thou Redeemer of the earth, / and manifest thy virgin-birth" is a later revision (*Hymns Ancient & Modern* and successors), cut to 5 stanzas, and recent editions may carry their own rights. Use Neale's 1851 wording below.

## The text

Latin in ecclesiastical spelling (v for consonantal u). **Bold** = stressed syllable. Neale's English is a verse paraphrase, not word-for-word; it runs on screen under the Latin.

### 1
> In**ten**de, qui **re**gis **Is**rael,
> **su**per **Che**rubim qui **se**des,
> ap**pa**re **E**phraem **co**ram, **ex**cita
> po**ten**tiam **tu**am et **ve**ni.

*Hear us, you who rule Israel,*
*enthroned above the Cherubim;*
*appear before Ephraim, stir up*
*your power, and come.* — translation: All Saints (from Psalm 80:1–2, which Ambrose versifies)

### 2
> **Ve**ni, re**demp**tor **gen**tium,
> os**ten**de **par**tum **vir**ginis;
> mi**re**tur **om**ne **sae**culum:
> **ta**lis **de**cet **par**tus **De**o.

*Come, Thou Redeemer of the earth,*
*Come, testify Thy Virgin-birth:*
*All lands admire, all times applaud:*
*Such is the birth that fits a God.*

### 3
> Non ex vi**ri**li **se**mine,
> sed **mys**tico spi**ra**mine
> **Ver**bum **De**i **fac**tum est **ca**ro,
> **fruc**tusque **ven**tris **flo**ruit.

*Begotten of no human will,*
*But of the Spirit, mystic still,*
*The Word of God, in flesh array'd,*
*The promis'd fruit to man display'd.*

### 4
> **Al**vus tu**mes**cit **vir**ginis,
> **claus**trum pu**do**ris **per**manet,
> ve**xil**la vir**tu**tum **mi**cant,
> ver**sa**tur in **tem**plo **De**us.

*The Virgin womb that burden gain'd,*
*With Virgin honour all unstain'd:*
*The banners there of virtue glow:*
*God in His temple dwells below.*

### 5
> Pro**ce**dat e **tha**lamo **su**o,
> pu**do**ris **au**la **re**gia,
> **ge**minae **gi**gas sub**stan**tiae
> **a**lacris ut **cur**rat **vi**am.

*Proceeding from His chamber free,*
*The royal hall of chastity,*
*Giant of twofold substance, straight*
*His destin'd way He runs elate.*

### 6
> E**gres**sus **e**ius a **Pa**tre,
> re**gres**sus **e**ius ad **Pa**trem;
> ex**cur**sus **us**que ad **in**feros,
> re**cur**sus ad **se**dem **De**i.

*From God the Father He proceeds:*
*To God the Father back He speeds:*
*Proceeds, as far as very hell;*
*Speeds back, to light ineffable.*

### 7
> Ae**qua**lis ae**ter**no **Pa**tri,
> **car**nis tro**pae**o **cin**gere,
> in**fir**ma **nos**tri **cor**poris
> vir**tu**te **fir**mans **per**peti.

*O Equal to Thy Father, Thou!*
*Gird on Thy fleshly mantle now:*
*The weakness of our mortal state*
*With deathless might invigorate.*

### 8
> Prae**se**pe iam **ful**get **tu**um,
> **lu**menque nox **spi**rat **no**vum,
> quod **nul**la nox in**ter**polet
> **fi**deque **iu**gi **lu**ceat.

*Thy cradle here shall glitter bright,*
*And darkness breathe a newer light;*
*Where endless faith shall shine serene,*
*And twilight never intervene.*

### Text notes
- Stanza 2, line 4: Fontaine reads ***Deo***; later breviaries have *Deum*. We follow Fontaine.
- Stanza 5: later breviaries have *Procedens de thalamo suo*; Fontaine's ***Procedat e thalamo suo*** is the older reading.
- Stanza 7: the Hymnology Archive web transcription has the OCR slips "aetemo" and "camis tropheo"; the correct words are ***aeterno*** and ***carnis tropaeo***.
- Neale's spellings ("array'd", "promis'd", "destin'd") are kept on screen as printed in 1851.

## Pronunciation (ecclesiastical / Italianate Latin)

Vowels always pure: a = *ah*, e = *eh*, i = *ee*, o = *oh*, u = *oo*; **ae** = *eh*.

## Suggested structure (~5 min)

Every word sung is Ambrose's; repeats are of whole stanzas only.

| Time | Section | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Intro | Low drone (D), cathedral air; a single horn or sax line, rubato |
| 0:30 | St. 1 *Intende* | Solo voice nearly unaccompanied: the psalm cry, Israel waiting |
| 1:00 | St. 2 *Veni, redemptor* | Harmony enters (bass, soft keys); the hymn's heart, slightly warmer |
| 1:30 | St. 3–4 | Voice over modal vamp; brushes enter quietly |
| 2:25 | Interlude | Instrumental; the sax answers the voice |
| 2:50 | St. 5–6 | The "giant runs his course": most motion, then St. 6 broadens |
| 3:50 | St. 7 | Pull back to voice and drone |
| 4:15 | St. 8 *Praesepe* | Light arrives: fullest voicing, open resolution |
| 4:45 | St. 2 again | Return of *Veni, redemptor gentium*, softer; fade on the drone |
