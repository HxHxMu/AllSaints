# Monetization plan (2026-10-28)

Goal: the channel pays its ~$20–60/mo costs, then grows revenue. Never paywall prayers; no guilt/urgency copy.

- **Phase 1 (0–2k followers):** saint web pages with affiliate "go deeper" links (Bookshop.org books; medals later), weekly email digest, support link. Support: **"Help keep the forgotten remembered."** / button **"Light a candle"** → Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/allholysaints, live). All Saints isn't a registered charity: say "support"/"tip", never "donation" or "tax-deductible". Stripe Payment Links later if needed.
- **Phase 2:** cross-posting — YouTube Shorts done (Shorts only; no long-form compilations yet); Facebook Reels possible later; Threads as a cheap later add-on.
- **Phase 3:** digital products (printable calendars, prayer cards), print-on-demand, **B2B daily-saint subscription for Catholic schools/parishes**.
- **Phase 4 (10k+):** sponsors, IG subscriptions.
