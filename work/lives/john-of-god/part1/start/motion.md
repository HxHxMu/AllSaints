# Part I · The Runaway · camera and motion prompts (image-to-video)

**Meta refuses shots with a child, so 1 and 4 stay stills; clips are 2, 5, 6, 7, 8** (made 2026-11-24 in Meta chat "Animating image to video", in the order 6, 8, 7, 5, 2). Save them as `live/N.mp4` in `work/lives/john-of-god/part1/live/`.

Add this line to the end of **every** prompt:
> One continuous shot, no cuts. 1920s silent-film camera: heavy, tripod-steady, slow and deliberate, no handheld shake, no drone or crane moves, no fast zoom. Keep the black-and-white film look, the grain and the faces exactly as in the image. Nobody speaks; no text appears.

### 1 · He leaves home (7.2 s) ★ · `shot1-start.png`
**Camera:** static wide shot.
**Motion:** the boy and the cloaked priest walk slowly away down the road; the boy looks back once toward the town, then turns and follows; their long shadows move ahead of them; mist drifts below the town.

### 4 · Left in Oropesa (6.8 s) ★ · `shot4-start.png`
**Camera:** very slow push in toward the boy.
**Motion:** the priest walks away into the distance along the road; the boy stands still and watches him go; the old shepherd rests a hand on his shoulder; the sheep graze and shift; wind stirs the grass and the clouds.

### 6 · The shepherd (6.0 s) ★ · `shot6-start.png`
**Camera:** very slow push in on his face.
**Motion:** he leans on his crook and gazes out across the plain, then slowly lowers his eyes; wind moves his hair and the sheepskin; sheep graze around him; mist drifts behind.

### 8 · He runs off to be a soldier (6.9 s) ★ · `shot8-start.png`
**Camera:** static.
**Motion:** he walks away down the moonlit road toward the distant campfires and tents without looking back; the crook stays leaning against the wall in the foreground; mist drifts low over the fields; the campfires flicker far away.
