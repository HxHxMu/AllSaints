### 4 · The pardon (4.7 s) · `shot4-start.png` · Flow

**Motion:** the officer on horseback holds up his hand; the soldier cuts the rope from the young man's wrists and steps back; the young man rubs his wrists and looks up at the officer, stunned; the horse shifts and tosses its head; mist drifts.
