# Lives of the Saints · Series 3 · St. Marguerite Bourgeoys (1620–1700)

Feast Jan 12 (died 12 Jan 1700). Canonized 31 Oct 1982 (John Paul II); beatified 1950. Patron: Congregation of Notre Dame, Canada's first teacher in Montreal.
Release (mid-Jan, monthly cadence): teaser Thu Jan 7, Story Sat Jan 9, reminder Sun Jan 10 8:30 PM; Parts I–VI Mon Jan 11 – Sat Jan 16 (Part II lands on her feast, Tue Jan 12), 7:30 PM ET; full 16:9 film Mon Jan 18.
Sources: Wikipedia; Vatican biography (1982 canonization); Catholic Encyclopedia, "Congregation of Notre Dame de Montreal" (1911). They disagree on dates (see Notes): keep only what at least two support, or attribute ("the Vatican biography says").

I · The Procession — Troyes, Champagne, born 17 April 1620, one of a large family; her mother dies while she is young (Vatican). On 7 Oct 1640, in a Rosary procession, she feels called to give herself to God (Vatican). She joins the Troyes sodality that teaches poor girls.
   Next: "the call."
II · The Call — 1642 she hears of the new colony of Ville-Marie (Montreal) (Vatican); 1652 she meets its governor, Maisonneuve, who invites her to teach there (W, V; CE says 1653). She says yes, and leaves.
   Next: "the crossing."
III · The Crossing — She sails for New France with about a hundred colonists (Wikipedia). The Vatican biography puts her arrival in Ville-Marie on 16 Nov 1653. A fragile fort-town on an island; no school yet, no pupils.
   Next: "the stable."
IV · The Stable — 1658 (W, V): Maisonneuve gives her a stone stable; she makes it a school, the first in Montreal. She teaches the settlers' children. (Mount Royal cross: Vatican biography only; attribute or omit.)
   Next: "the daughters."
V · The Daughters — She receives girls arriving in the colony (the filles du roi: W, V) and founds a community of women teachers who do not live behind cloister walls. 1670–72 she travels back to France for letters patent and companions (CE; W 1671). 
   Next: "the rule."
VI · The Rule — Her community is recognised: rule and constitutions accepted in 1698 (CE, V). She dies in Montreal on 12 January 1700, at 79. Canonized 1982. "Saint Marguerite Bourgeoys, pray for us."
