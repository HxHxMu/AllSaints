# Marguerite Part III · The Crossing · camera and motion prompts

Clips: **1, 3, 4, 7, 8**. Stills: 2, 5, 6. All adults, no children. Add the standard silent-film suffix to every prompt (see Part I's motion.md).

### 3 · Only the sea · static. She sits on the chest with folded hands as the lantern swings and the shadows move; she slowly lifts her eyes.
### 4 · Ville-Marie · very slow push in. The ship glides slowly toward the shore; mist drifts; she lifts her chin and gazes ahead.
### 7 · She waits · static. She stands on the shore beside her bundle, looking at the empty settlement; mist drifts; she slowly picks up the bundle.
### 8 · Years pass · static. Mist drifts across the water; she stands still at the end of the pier, then slowly lowers her head.
