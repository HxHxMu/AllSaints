# Marguerite Part 5 · camera and motion prompts (image-to-video)

Clips: 1, 3, 5, 7, 8. All adults, no children. Standard silent-film suffix as in Part I's motion.md.

### 1
Camera: static. Motion: she lowers her hand and watches the ship come slowly through the mist; the women behind her stand still; water ripples.

### 3
Camera: static. Motion: the two women sit together; the older one gently pats the younger one's hand; the candle flickers.

### 5
Camera: very slow push in. Motion: snow drifts outside; the seated women sew quietly; she looks out at the street and slowly nods.

### 7
Camera: very slow push in. Motion: she reads the document, then slowly lowers it to her chest and closes her eyes; the candle flickers.

### 8
