# Part IV · The Habit · camera and motion prompts (image-to-video)

**For every clip:** upload the start frame, paste its prompt, **9:16**, the longest length the tool allows (extra is trimmed). No sound needed. Name each clip by its shot number (`1.mp4` … `10.mp4`) and drop it in `work/nocturnes/part4/live/`.

Add this line to the end of **every** prompt, so the tool doesn't modernise the look:
> One continuous shot, no cuts. 1920s silent-film camera: heavy, tripod-steady, slow and deliberate, no handheld shake, no drone or crane moves, no fast zoom. Keep the black-and-white film look, the grain and the faces exactly as in the image. Nobody speaks; no text appears.

Shots 3, 6, 8 are the ones to make first (Margaret moves). The other seven work as stills; animate them if you have credits left.

### 1 · The habit on the table (4.7 s) · `shot1-start.jpg`
**Camera:** very slow push in toward the folded habit.
**Motion:** the shaft of light slowly brightens across the table and the folded cloth; dust drifts in the beam.

### 2 · Three years (4.3 s) · `shot2-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static wide shot of the cloister.
**Motion:** time passes: the shadows of the columns sweep slowly across the stones as the light moves from morning to evening; a few dry leaves drift across the floor in the wind.

### 3 · The gossips (5.6 s) ★ · `shot3-start.jpg`
**Camera:** slowly pulls back ahead of her as she walks toward it.
**Motion:** she walks slowly up the street with her eyes lowered; at the well the women lean closer together, whisper behind their hands and turn their heads to follow her; mist drifts low along the cobbles.

### 4 · The whispering shadows (4.2 s) · `shot4-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static.
**Motion:** the two huge shadows on the wall lean toward each other and away again as the women whisper; one shadow's hand rises to cover a mouth; the light flickers faintly.

### 5 · Her prayer (4.3 s) · `shot5-start.jpg`
**Camera:** very slow push in on the joined hands.
**Motion:** the hands stay joined and still; the candle flame flickers and its light breathes softly over the fingers and the wood.

### 6 · Sending her son (4.0 s) ★ · `shot6-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static.
**Motion:** she holds the boy tightly for a moment, then slowly lets him go and touches his face; he turns and walks to the friar, who puts a hand on his shoulder; they walk away through the gate while she stays kneeling and watches.

### 7 · The young friar (3.7 s) · `shot7-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static, then a very slow push in.
**Motion:** the young friar stands still in the bright doorway, then slowly turns his head toward the light; his habit stirs slightly; light flares softly around his silhouette.

### 8 · The habit (6.3 s) ★ · `shot8-start.jpg`
**Camera:** slow push in on her face.
**Motion:** the friar lowers the folded habit into her hands; she draws it slowly to her chest and bows her head over it; the candles flicker; her expression is peaceful.

### 9 · The crucifix (5.1 s) · `shot9-start.jpg`
**Camera:** very slow push in toward the crucifix.
**Motion:** the light from above slowly grows brighter on the crucifix; the candle flame grows still and tall.

### 10 · The shoot in the stones (6.4 s) · `shot10-start.jpg`
**Camera:** very slow push in, low to the floor.
**Motion:** the little shoot's leaves tremble and slowly open toward the shaft of light; dust drifts in the beam.
