### 1 · The friars listen (5.8 s) · `shot1-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static, then a very slow push in toward the friar's back.
**Motion:** the friar in the brown habit sits still, head bowed slightly as he listens; the woman's shadow on the cloister wall moves a little as she speaks and gestures; dust drifts in the shaft of light.

### 2 · Nursing the sick (5.0 s) ★ · `shot2-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static, medium shot.
**Motion:** she wrings the cloth out over the basin, water dripping, then leans forward and lays it gently on the old woman's forehead; the old woman's eyes close; the candle flame flickers and its light moves softly over both faces.

### 3 · The straw mat (6.3 s) · `shot3-start.jpg`
**Camera:** very slow push in toward the mat.
**Motion:** nothing moves but the light: moonlight from the high window slowly shifts across the floor; a little dust drifts through the beam.

### 4 · Not enough (2.9 s) · `shot4-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static, close.
**Motion:** rain runs down the window and drips from the sill; the candle flame gutters, shrinks and leans in a draught; its light flickers on the wall.

### 5 · Into the church (5.8 s) ★ · `shot5-start.jpg`
**Camera:** behind her, slowly following as she walks forward.
**Motion:** she walks slowly through the tall doors into the church, the rope cord at her neck; one by one the people in the pews turn their heads toward her; shafts of light fall across the aisle; incense haze drifts.

### 6 · The cord (3.0 s) · `shot6-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static close-up, then an almost imperceptible push in on her hands.
**Motion:** her clasped hands tighten slowly around the loose end of the cord; her chest rises and falls as she breathes; she lowers her eyes; the hard side light trembles faintly.

### 7 · Before them all (5.7 s) ★ · `shot7-start.jpg`
**Camera:** slow push in on her face.
**Motion:** she kneels with her hands joined and bows her head lower, her lips moving silently as she asks forgiveness; a tear catches the light; behind her the faces in the pews stay still in shadow.

### 8 · Not everyone believed (2.7 s) · `shot8-start.jpg`
**Camera:** slow pan along the row of faces.
**Motion:** the faces in the pews stay stern and still; one by one some of them turn their heads away; candlelight flickers across them.
