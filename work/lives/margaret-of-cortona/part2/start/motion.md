# Part II · camera and motion prompts (image-to-video)

**For every clip:** upload the start frame, paste its prompt, **9:16**, the longest length the tool allows (each shot needs 3–6 s on screen; extra is trimmed). No sound needed.

Add this line to the end of **every** prompt, so the tool doesn't modernise the look:
> One continuous shot, no cuts. 1920s silent-film camera: heavy, tripod-steady, slow and deliberate, no handheld shake, no drone or crane moves, no fast zoom. Keep the black-and-white film look, the grain and the faces exactly as in the image. Nobody speaks; no text appears.

Shots 2, 4, 5, 8 are the ones to make first (Margaret moves). 1, 3, 6, 7 work as stills already; animate them only if you have credits left.

### 1 · The closed door (on screen 4.2 s) · `shot1-start.jpg`
**Camera:** very slow push in toward the door, straight on.
**Motion:** the woman's shadow on the door shifts slightly as she breathes and shifts the child on her hip; thin mist drifts along the ground; the light fades a little, as at dusk.

### 2 · Coming home (4.7 s) · `shot2-start.jpg` ★
**Camera:** locked-off wide shot, then a slow push in as she approaches.
**Motion:** she walks slowly up the path toward the camera carrying the boy on her hip; her cloak and veil move in the wind; mist drifts across the path; her face stays calm and tired.

### 3 · The stepmother (3.3 s) · `shot3-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static, low angle.
**Motion:** the woman in the doorway slowly tightens her grip on the door frame and lifts her chin slightly; her expression stays cold and still; the light from outside flickers faintly on her face.

### 4 · Turned away (3.4 s) · `shot4-start.jpg` ★
**Camera:** static; at the end a very slight push in on her face.
**Motion:** the old man lowers his eyes and slowly closes the heavy wooden door; the strip of light from inside narrows on her face as it shuts; she holds the boy closer and does not move.

### 5 · The fig tree (5.4 s) · `shot5-start.jpg` ★
**Camera:** very slow push in, almost imperceptible.
**Motion:** she weeps quietly, her shoulders trembling, hand over her face; the boy sleeps in her lap; the fig branches sway gently in the night wind and their moonlit shadows move across her.

### 6 · The old life (5.0 s) · `shot6-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static wide shot, then a slow push in past her shoulder toward the distant castle.
**Motion:** she stands still at the crossroads with the sleeping child, seen from behind; her cloak stirs in the wind; the castle windows glow on the hill; clouds drift slowly over the moon.

### 7 · The road to Cortona (6.2 s) · `shot7-start.jpg`
**Camera:** static wide shot, held; the figures do the moving.
**Motion:** she walks away from the camera up the long road holding the boy's hand, slowly getting smaller; dawn light grows over the ridge and their long shadows stretch back toward the camera.

### 8 · The open door (4.8 s) · `shot8-start.jpg` ★
**Camera:** slow push in toward the doorway, past Margaret's shoulder.
**Motion:** the two women in the doorway reach out and take her hands; warm candlelight flickers and spills over her face and the cobbles; the boy looks up at them; her expression softens.
