# Firefly test: Part I as live action, in the manner of a 1920s German silent film

Settings for every clip: **9:16 vertical**, the longest length Firefly allows (we need 4–8 s per scene; short clips can be slowed or held). No sound needed: the narration and score are laid on afterwards.

Paste the **look** line at the start of every prompt so the clips match each other. Use the **Margaret** line word for word wherever she appears, so she looks like the same woman from shot to shot.

**Look:**
> Black-and-white 1920s German Expressionist silent film, orthochromatic film stock, extreme chiaroscuro, one hard light source, deep black shadows, painted theatrical sets with skewed angular architecture, drifting mist, visible film grain and slight flicker, slow deliberate camera. Thirteenth-century Italy. Solemn, reverent, never horror, no gore.

**Margaret:**
> a young Italian woman in her twenties with a pale oval face and dark eyes, wearing a long dark wool gown and a hooded dark cloak with a light linen veil

### 1. The hound (7 s)
> [Look] A lean grey hunting hound walks slowly alone through the arched gateway of a stone castle at dusk, bright mist behind it, its long shadow stretching across the courtyard toward the camera. It stops and lifts its head. Static camera, slow push in.

### 2. Margaret (8 s)
> [Look] [Margaret], about seventeen, steps out of the open door of a rough stone farmhouse into hard side light, steep stylized hills behind her. She stops and looks out across the valley. Slow push in.

### 3. Little warmth (7 s)
> [Look] A small girl of seven in a plain medieval dress sits alone on a wooden stool beside a dying hearth fire in a bare farmhouse room. The tall shadow of a standing woman slowly rises across the plastered wall above her; the woman herself is never seen. Static camera.

### 4. The night ride (6 s)
> [Look] At night a young nobleman gallops on a dark horse with [Margaret] riding behind him, her veil streaming, along a crooked moonlit road climbing toward a jagged hilltop castle. Wide shot, the camera pans with them.

### 5. Nine years (5.5 s)
> [Look] [Margaret] stands at a tall narrow castle window in a dark stone chamber, holding a small boy of about three in her arms, a single shaft of pale light across them. She rocks him gently and looks out of the window. Slow push in.

### 6. The hound returns (8.5 s)
> [Look] At night a lean grey hunting hound leads [Margaret], who carries a small iron lantern, into a forest of tall, thin, sharply leaning trees; shafts of white light cut through thick mist; the lantern is the only warm light. They walk away from the camera along a narrow path. Tracking shot behind them.

### 7. She found him (5.7 s)
> [Look] In a misty forest clearing [Margaret] kneels with her hands over her face; the hound sits beside her. Beyond her a cloaked man lies still on the ground, almost lost in shadow and mist. No blood, no wound visible. Static camera, very slow push in.

### 8. Prayer (4.3 s)
> [Look] [Margaret] kneels in a small bare stone chapel at night, her hands joined in prayer, facing a plain stone altar with one candle burning beneath a simple wooden crucifix. Candlelight flickers on her face. Static camera.
